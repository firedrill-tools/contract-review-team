# Meridian Services Agreement (synthetic)

## C1 Renewal
The subscription renews for twelve months unless the customer gives renewal notice at least 30 days before expiration.

## C2 Termination
Either party may terminate at expiration only if it gives termination notice at least 60 days before expiration.

## C3 Incident notice
The vendor must notify the customer of a confirmed data breach within 72 hours of confirmation.

## C4 Payment
The customer must pay each undisputed invoice within 30 days of receipt.

## C5 Service level
The vendor must maintain 99.9% monthly uptime and provide service credits for a missed target.

## C6 Data deletion
The vendor must delete customer data within 15 days after contract termination and provide a deletion certificate.
